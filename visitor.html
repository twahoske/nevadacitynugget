Trails & Vistas

Trail of the Day · Deer Creek Tribute Trail: Nisenan Bridge

Jordan St. trailhead (walk from Broad & Pine via the Pine St. bridge) · 1.5-mile loop · lower trail drops 185 ft · dirt · Easy to moderate

Walk from Broad and Pine across the Pine Street bridge to the Jordan Street trailhead, then loop down to the Angkula Seo suspension bridge over Deer Creek, past the 1850 Rough & Ready Ditch and Nisenan history signs. About a mile and a half.

More hikes, trailheads, and current closures: Trails & Vistas, the Nugget's walker's guide. Conditions change; call Bear Yuba Land Trust, (530) 272-5994 before you go. Source: Bear Yuba Land Trust.

Spotlight on Seven Hills

Nevada City's Other Business District

About a mile south of Broad Street, tucked off Zion Street behind SPD Market, the Seven Hills District is where Nevada City does its errands, and a good deal more. The Chamber of Commerce counts an arthouse movie theater, artisan food shops, casual places to eat, and more than 35 health practitioners along Zion Street, Argall Way, Searls Avenue and Providence Mine Road.
