Trails & Vistas

Trail of the Day · Cascade Canal Trail

Gracie Rd., 700 ft below Banner-Lava Cap Rd.; or Red Dog Rd. · 4.5 miles one way · 90 ft · dirt · Easy grade, long

Four and a half nearly level miles along a canal on Banner Mountain, under fir and dogwood, with a bench near the red sluice gate that looks down on St. Canice's spire and the courthouse. Start at the Gracie Road lot.

More hikes, trailheads, and current closures: Trails & Vistas, the Nugget's walker's guide. Conditions change, and winter rain can leave dirt trails muddy; call Bear Yuba Land Trust, (530) 272-5994 before you go. Source: Bear Yuba Land Trust.

Spotlight on Seven Hills

Nevada City's Other Business District

About a mile south of Broad Street, tucked off Zion Street behind SPD Market, the Seven Hills District is where Nevada City does its errands, and a good deal more. The Chamber of Commerce counts an arthouse movie theater, artisan food shops, casual places to eat, and more than 35 health practitioners along Zion Street, Argall Way, Searls Avenue and Providence Mine Road.
