Trails & Vistas

Trail of the Day · Government Center Trails & Tobiassen Park

Rood Center, Hwy 49 at N. Bloomfield Rd. · 0.6 mile plus 0.2 in the park · 20 to 60 ft · paved and dirt · Easy

A quick history walk at the Rood Center: hydraulic mining monitors on display, a plaque on American Hill, and Charles Marsh Pond, an 1861 reservoir in Tobiassen Park with piers and picnic tables. Under a mile; good for a lunch break.

More hikes, trailheads, and current closures: Trails & Vistas, the Nugget's walker's guide. Conditions change; call Nevada County Public Works, (530) 265-1411 before you go. Source: Bear Yuba Land Trust.
