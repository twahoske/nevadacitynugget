How the Count Works

One copy per reader per day. Each phone or computer that opens today's paper at nevadacitynugget.com counts once per day (Pacific time), however often it comes back. So the total is copies read, not separate people: a regular who reads every morning adds one a day, and someone reading on both phone and laptop counts twice.

What counts. Only today's front page counts. Archived editions and the other pages don't.

Wired is how many times readers sent the whole paper to a friend with "☞ Wire The Nugget to a Friend."

What's kept. Only the date and those two numbers. The counter never records who read, their address, or their device; see Privacy.
