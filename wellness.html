Tonics, Elixirs & Tinctures

Bottled, brewed and steeped right here in town. Not one drop from a traveling wagon.

  • Remedy Garden412 Commercial St.

    Organic herbal apothecary · (530) 470-6157

    Jars, tins and bundles aplenty. Grandmother's remedy cabinet, if Grandmother had gone into business.

    Shelves of "high quality medicinal herbs, tinctures, essential oils," as the shop puts it, plus tea blends, elixirs, medicinal mushrooms, salves, balms, incense and resins, all "organic and sustainably wildcrafted." Its herbalists teach classes and workshops on herbalism, too.

    Pay a call: Shop and class calendar at remedygarden.com. Source: remedygarden.com.

  • Elixart203 York St.

    Alcohol-free elixir bar since 2012

    A saloon where the bartender asks how your spirit is, and means the other kind.

    "Alcohol-free elixirs, kava, jun and tea," plus plant-spirit drink mixes, mushroom blends and tinctures. It began in 2012 as a five-seat tea bar inside a Nevada City art gallery and now bills itself as "nightlife without alcohol at the center," with a mobile bar for weddings and festivals.

    Pay a call: Menu, hours and events at elixart.com. Source: elixart.com; Holbrooke Hotel wellness list.

  • Jade Mountain TeaJade Valley, about 8 minutes from downtown

    Tea school & cultural center · by booking only · (530) 264-6954

    Tea poured slowly enough that you may forget what you were in a hurry about.

    A private retreat for classical Asian tea culture and Zen meditation, led by tea and Zen teacher MJ Greenmountain: Gong Fu tea ceremonies with antique tea wares, Zen & Tea micro-sessions and mini-retreats, and Asian tea classes. The grounds are "digi-free," so leave the telephone in the buggy.

    Pay a call: Check availability before booking at jademountaintea.com. Source: jademountaintea.com.

Limbering Parlors

Yoga, Pilates and breathing, where the town goes to touch its toes.

  • Inner Path Yoga & Meditation200 Commercial St.

    Open daily 10 AM to 6 PM, as posted · (530) 470-6057

    Pay what the spirit moves you to. The Nugget suggests more than a nickel.

    "Resources for yoga, meditation and a spiritual life," run by a nonprofit (Valley Light Center, doing business as Inner Path). Classes include Bhakti Yoga Flow, Kundalini, Gentle and Restorative yoga, and Qi Gong held outdoors at Robinson Plaza when the weather allows. Several classes are by donation; others are drop-in or sliding scale.

    Pay a call: Class calendar at nevadacity.innerpath.org. Source: nevadacity.innerpath.org.

  • Heart & Soul Pilates & Bodycare Studio580 Searls Ave.

    (530) 277-7299

    Contraptions of springs and straps that would have alarmed a Victorian, used here strictly for good.

    A Pilates studio for "every age, background, and fitness level," its listing says, built on strength, flexibility and "inner balance" with personal instruction.

    Pay a call: Schedule at heartandsoulpilates.com. Source: Nevada City Chamber listing.

Restoratives for Body & Spirit

Oils, steams, facials and the laying on of hands.

  • The Ayurvedic Day Spa at the California College of Ayurveda700 Zion St.

    (530) 478-9100 · book online

    Warm oil poured on the forehead. Our forefathers would have called it a scandal; we call it Tuesday.

    Traditional Ayurvedic treatments to "restore your sense of balance and well-being": full-body oil massage, a warm oil stream over the forehead, herbal steam, marma therapy, raw-silk glove exfoliation, and an Ayurvedic facial. It also offers stays of "four to thirty-day" retreats with private cottages and prepared meals.

    Pay a call: Treatments and booking at ayurvedacollege.com. Source: ayurvedacollege.com; Holbrooke Hotel wellness list.

  • The Golden Bee Holistic Center310 1/2 Broad St.

    By appointment, Mon-Fri 9:30 AM to 6 PM, weekends available, as posted · (530) 264-7383

    The only shop on Broad Street where you can buy local honey and have your stars read in one stop.

    A holistic center offering, by its own list, health coaching, reflexology, Reiki, counseling, hypnotherapy, Emotional Freedom Technique, meditation instruction and transpersonal astrology, "and more." Its gift store sells local art, painted stones, tiles, resin art and locally sourced honey.

    Pay a call: Services at goldenbeeholisticcenter.com. Source: goldenbeeholisticcenter.com; Nevada City Chamber listing.

  • Seen Skincare204 N. Pine St.

    (415) 745-0826

    Botanicals pressed cold, applied warmly.

    Facials "crafted in-house from cold pressed organic botanicals and oils," blended fresh for each appointment, with gua sha and other treatments.

    Pay a call: Booking at seenskincare.com. Source: seenskincare.com; Holbrooke Hotel wellness list.

  • Namaste MadreNevada City

    (530) 448-2745

    Bring your own drum, or make one. Ceremony provided.

    "Shamanic Energy Healing, Motivational Teachings, Sound Healing, Gatherings, Ceremonial Circles, Nutrition, Yoga, & more," in its own words, with shamanic circle gatherings, a drum-making ceremony, breathwork and guided meditations.

    Pay a call: Gatherings at namastemadre.com. Source: namastemadre.com.

Stones, Crystals & Curiosities

For the shelf, the pocket, or the windowsill that catches the morning.

  • Crystal Rainbow Rock Shop310 Commercial St.

    (530) 687-2323

    In a gold town, the rocks were always the point. Some of these just happen to be prettier.

    A small rock and mineral shop in a cabin on Commercial Street: crystals, minerals, fossils, geodes and polished stones, with gifts at a range of prices.

    Pay a call: No website found; stop in. Source: Giftly listing.

  • Rubicon Crystals312 Broad St.

    Thu-Sun noon to 5 PM, as listed · 805-296-0955

    Polished, rough, or rare. The Nugget has no opinion on your aura but approves of your taste.

    Affordable crystals, gemstones, minerals and jewelry, its Chamber listing says: carvings, specimens, polished and rough stones, rare pieces and stones found locally.

    Pay a call: Stop in Thursday through Sunday. Source: Nevada City Chamber listing.

  • Omari318 Broad St.

    Wed-Sun 11 AM to 4 PM, as listed

    A sacred space with a shop counter. The medicine shows had the counter; Omari added the sacred.

    "More than a store," its Chamber listing says, "a sacred space where creativity, community" and more come together: ritual craft goods and local artisan wares, with visiting artists, musicians and teachers.

    Pay a call: Stop in Wednesday through Sunday afternoons. Source: Nevada City Chamber listing; Chamber member page.

Handicrafts for the Nerves

The oldest cure for a modern fret: get your hands in some clay.

  • Wolf Craft Pottery Studio & Art Cafe244 Commercial St.

    Opened fall 2026 · classes also at The Tinnery, 205C York St. · 530-557-3727

    The finest nerve tonic the Nugget knows: a lump of wet clay and nowhere to be.

    Owner Leslie Barbazette's studio has nine pottery wheels, three kilns and an art cafe pouring coffee, tea, spritzers, beer and wine. Six-week wheel and hand-building classes, a Friday date-night wheel class, teen hand-building, and memberships for those who'd rather throw clay of an afternoon, as The Union reported in October.

    Pay a call: Classes at wolfcraftcollective.com. Source: The Union, Oct. 10, 2026; wolfcraftcollective.com.

The Licensed Herb

Lawful, state licensed, 21 and over, ID in hand. Checked against the state's own rolls.

  • Elevation 2477'569 Searls Ave., Suite D

    State retail license C10-0000138-LIC, active · daily 9 AM to 9 PM, as posted · (530) 264-7684

    Named for the town's elevation in feet. The Nugget makes no further jokes about getting high in Nevada City.

    Nevada City's licensed cannabis retailer, for adult use and medicinal customers. Adults 21 and over with valid ID; California does not honor out-of-state medical cards. Online orders for express pickup.

    Pay a call: Menu at elevationcannabis.co. Source: Department of Cannabis Control license record; elevationcannabis.co.

Up the Ridge & Down the Hill

Worth the buggy ride.

  • The Expanding Light Retreat at Ananda Village13930 Expanding Light Dr., up the San Juan Ridge

    Year-round personal retreats

    A silent retreat: the one place in Nevada County nobody will ask about parking.

    Yoga and meditation retreats inspired by Paramhansa Yogananda and Swami Kriyananda, "nestled in a vibrant spiritual community." Book a personal retreat with flexible dates, or a program; December lists a first-Kriya course and a supported silent retreat.

    Pay a call: Programs at meditationretreat.org. Source: meditationretreat.org.

  • Wild Mountain Yoga Center121 S. Church St., Grass Valley

    Above Yuba Blue

    Down the hill, up the stairs, and into the pose.

    Owner-director Amanda Sarene Dozal teaches a "Creative Hatha Blend," along with Medicinal Movement, Somatic Sundays and therapeutic private sessions. She has taught since 2001.

    Pay a call: Schedule at wildmtyoga.com. Source: wildmtyoga.com.

  • MushBarnInside Make Local Habit, 112 W. Main St., Grass Valley

    Farm visits by appointment

    Mushrooms, honestly labeled. In this town, that counts as a public service.

    A Grass Valley mushroom farm and maker: sun-grown fresh mushrooms from a barn lab and greenhouse, mushroom products, foraged native Sierra mushrooms in the rainy season, and classes on fungi.

    Pay a call: Shop and visits at mushbarn.com. Source: Healer Hive listing.

A Word From the Wagon
