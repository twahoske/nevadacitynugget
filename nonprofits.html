Arts, Music & Stage

The stages, the station and the bands that keep the town humming.

  • Miners Foundry Cultural Center325 Spring St.

    Box office Tue-Fri, 10 AM to 3:30 PM, as posted · office (530) 265-5040

    The old foundry on Spring Street, now a historic nonprofit venue for concerts, plays, dances, weddings and community gatherings, "serving Nevada County since 1859," as it puts it.

    Lend a hand: Give through its Make a Gift page or volunteer. Source: minersfoundry.org.

  • KVMR 89.5 FM120 Bridge St.

    Community radio since 1978 · (530) 265-9555

    Independent, listener-supported community radio, on the air since July 14, 1978, with music, news and public affairs. It reaches well beyond town on translators and online. Its stated aim: to give community members a way to connect through programs that "entertain, inform, and educate."

    Lend a hand: Become a member or give at kvmr.org. Source: Big Day of Giving profile; Wikipedia.

    Disclosure: The Nugget's publisher co-hosts a KVMR show, Under the Influence.

  • Nevada Theatre401 Broad St.

    Opened Sept. 11, 1865 · (530) 265-6161

    California's oldest theater building still in operation, the theater says, where Mark Twain and Jack London once appeared. A movie house from 1909, dark from 1957, and reopened by public donation in 1968, it hosts plays, concerts and films year round under the Nevada Theatre Commission.

    Lend a hand: Donations go to restoring and preserving the building; see nevadatheatre.com. Source: nevadatheatre.com.

  • Sierra StagesPlays at the Nevada Theatre and around the county

    Community theater since 2008

    A nonprofit community theater company, incorporated in 2008 and tax-exempt since 2009, out to "enrich the cultural life of Nevada County" with affordable, high-quality shows at the Nevada Theatre, the Miners Foundry, Off Center Stage, the Center for the Arts and the North Star House.

    Lend a hand: Season, tickets and giving at sierrastages.org. Source: sierrastages.org.

  • Nevada City Film FestivalDowntown Nevada City each June

    26th festival ran June 26-28, 2026 · back June 25-27, 2027

    An independent film festival led since 2001 by co-founder Jeff Clark, with a Filmed in Nevada County grant that backs local filmmakers and crews.

    Lend a hand: Volunteer or sponsor through the festival. Source: nevadacityfilmfestival.com; festival people page.

  • Nevada County Arts Council100 N. Pine St., Suite C

    State-Local Partner with the California Arts Council

    The county's arts agency, a 501(c)(3) that works to "promote and sustain the visual, literary and performing arts of Nevada County," with grants, artist calls and the Grass Valley-Nevada City Cultural District.

    Lend a hand: Donate, join or volunteer at nevadacountyarts.org. Source: nevadacountyarts.org.

  • Nevada County Concert BandParks and parades in Nevada City and Grass Valley

    Free concerts by volunteer musicians

    Brings back the old Sunday band concert, the band says: a summer Picnic Pops series, the Fourth of July parade, the county fair, and a Constitution Day street concert.

    Lend a hand: Players and listeners welcome; contact the band through the Chamber listing. Source: Nevada City Chamber member directory.

History & Heritage

Gold Rush brick, narrow-gauge iron, and the people who were here first.

  • Nevada County Historical Society & Searls Historical Library161 Nevada City Hwy.

    Library Mon-Sat, 1 to 4 PM, as posted · (530) 265-5910

    Since 1944 the Society has worked "to preserve every source of Nevada County history." Its archive, the Searls Historical Library, holds more than 30,000 photographs, nearly 3,000 books, thousands of maps, and old journals, ledgers and county files. Free.

    Lend a hand: Join, renew or volunteer on scanning projects through nevadacountyhistory.org. Source: nevadacountyhistory.org; Nevada City Chamber member directory.

  • Firehouse No. 1 Museum214 Main St.

    May-Oct, Wed-Sun 1 to 4 PM · winter by appointment for groups of six or more

    The Historical Society's museum in the 1861 firehouse, open since 1947, with Nisenan cultural items, temple altars from Grass Valley's Chinatown, Donner Party relics and a Victorian parlor upstairs. Kept going by visitor donations.

    Lend a hand: Drop something in the box when it reopens; see the museum page. Source: nevadacountyhistory.org.

  • Nevada County Narrow Gauge Railroad Museum5 Kidder Ct.

    Sat-Sun 10 AM to 4 PM; Mon, Tue, Fri 10 AM to 2 PM, as posted · free · (530) 470-0902

    Home of Engine 5, an 1875 Baldwin, plus wooden rail cars, an early steam automobile and a restoration shop where volunteers keep the rolling stock running. Railbus rides on Saturdays April through October.

    Lend a hand: Donate, buy a tie, or join the Friends at ncngrrmuseum.org. Source: ncngrrmuseum.org; Historical Society contacts.

  • HUṠWEJP.O. Box 2624, Nevada City

    Formerly CHIRP (California Heritage: Indigenous Research Project)

    A Tribally-guided nonprofit supporting the Nevada City Rancheria Nisenan Tribe, with land rematriation, cultural revitalization and public education. Its line: "Restoring Culture to landscape, and landscape to Culture."

    Lend a hand: Give once or monthly through the Ancestral Homelands Reciprocity Program, or volunteer, at huswej.org. Source: huswej.org.

  • 'Uba Seo: Nisenan Arts & Culture225 Broad St.

    Thu & Sun 11 AM to 5 PM; Fri & Sat 10 AM to 5 PM, as posted · free · 530-955-5134

    HUṠWEJ's contemporary Indigenous art gallery and culture center, directed by Nisenan artists and perspectives. Hours shift with Tribal needs, so call ahead.

    Lend a hand: See current shows at ubaseo.org. Source: ubaseo.org.

Rivers, Forests & Trails

Clean water, healthy woods, and somewhere to ride.

  • South Yuba River Citizens League (SYRCL)313 Railroad Ave., Suite 101

    Mon-Fri 9 AM to 5 PM · (530) 265-5961

    The Yuba River's watchdog and restorer, working on rivers, meadows and forests, with salmon expeditions and field science for kids, and host of the Wild & Scenic Film Festival (the 25th runs Feb. 18-22, 2027). Its vision: a Yuba watershed that "thrives and is resilient in the face of climate change."

    Lend a hand: Volunteer, join or donate at yubariver.org. Source: yubariver.org.

  • Sierra Streams Institute117 New Mohawk Rd., Suite H

    Founded 1995 as Friends of Deer Creek · (530) 470-6037

    Citizen science, community engagement and education: volunteers sample water, bugs, algae and wildlife, and the institute restores creeks and forests and runs the California Naturalist course. Deer Creek is still its home watershed.

    Lend a hand: Volunteer, intern, or give at sierrastreamsinstitute.org. Source: sierrastreamsinstitute.org.

  • Yuba Watershed InstituteNevada City

    Stewards about 2,000 acres of the 'Inimim Forest

    A 501(c)(3) "promoting sustainable use of natural resources and protecting long-term biological diversity in the Yuba River watershed." Its flagship is the 'Inimim Forest on the San Juan Ridge, managed with the Bureau of Land Management, plus an annual Fungus Foray and seasonal walks and talks.

    Lend a hand: Join or donate at yubawatershedinstitute.org. Source: yubawatershedinstitute.org.

  • Bicyclists of Nevada CountyP.O. Box 1311, Nevada City

    Mountain-bike trail association

    A 501(c)(3) "committed to responsible mountain biking," with group rides and volunteer trail-building and maintenance days.

    Lend a hand: Find a workday or ride through its Trailforks listing. Source: Trailforks directory.

Neighbors & Kids

For children in court, neighbors in a dispute, readers, and everyone in between.

  • Child Advocates of Nevada County200 Providence Mine Rd., Suites 119 & 210

    Mon-Fri 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM · 530-265-9550

    Trains volunteer CASAs, Court Appointed Special Advocates, to stand up for abused and neglected children in court, and runs Healthy Babies home visits for new families and One 2 One mentoring for ages 10 to 17. "We advocate for the wellbeing of children by disrupting the cycles of abuse."

    Lend a hand: Become a CASA or give at caofnc.org. Source: caofnc.org.

  • Conflict Resolution Center of Nevada County308 Main St., Suite 5

    (530) 477-6517, voice or text

    A nonprofit that supplies trained mediators to help neighbors, families and businesses settle disputes, and trains new mediators.

    Lend a hand: See resolveconflicts.org. Source: resolveconflicts.org.

  • Sierra Services for the Blind546 Searls Ave.

    530-265-2121

    Free help for people losing their sight in Nevada and Placer counties, for more than four decades: rides to medical appointments, peer groups, a library of visual aids, and advocacy.

    Lend a hand: Call to volunteer as a driver or to give. Source: Nevada City Chamber member directory; Idealist.

  • Nevada County PrideWestern Nevada County

    Free to join

    A 501(c)(3) social network of LGBTQ+ people and allies, with a book club, meetups, potlucks and advocacy all year.

    Lend a hand: Volunteer or donate at nevadacountypride.org. Source: nevadacountypride.org.

  • Friends of the Nevada County LibrariesP.O. Box 23, Nevada City

    Since 1971 · 530-557-0119

    Raises money for the county libraries' books, equipment and programs through memberships and used-book sales, sorted by volunteers known as the Bookies.

    Lend a hand: Join or find the next book sale at ncfol.org. Source: ncfol.org; YubaNet, Oct. 14, 2022.

Lodges, Clubs & Crews

The old fraternal halls, the service club, the ball club and the people who come find you in the snow.

  • Rotary Club of Nevada CityMeets at 420 Providence Mine Rd.

    Since Oct. 3, 1935 · Thursdays at 12:15 PM, with evening socials

    The town's service club, with Dolly Parton's Imagination Library (free books for kids up to age five), Pumpkins Palooza, polio eradication, and Project Warmth coat drives with United Way.

    Lend a hand: Visit a meeting or ask about joining at nevadacityrotary.org. Source: nevadacityrotary.org.

  • Elks Lodge No. 518P.O. Box 611, Nevada City

    Benevolent and Protective Order of Elks

    The Nevada City lodge of the Elks, which by its federal filings backs veterans, scholarships, youth programs, and therapy and preschool vision screenings for children in need.

    Lend a hand: Ask a member about joining or the lodge's charity work. Source: Cause IQ, from IRS filings.

  • Oustomah Lodge No. 16, Odd FellowsOdd Fellows Hall, between Broad and Spring streets

    Hall rentals 530-362-4888

    Friendship, Love and Truth, in a hall with doors on both Broad and Spring. Its Good Works committee backs Meals on Wheels, the Red Cross, Sierra Roots and the Kare Crisis Nursery; it also runs the Seven Stars Gallery, a Youth Arts Program, concerts, dances, and a first-Tuesday potluck at 5:30 PM.

    Lend a hand: Come to a potluck, rent the hall, or ask about joining at nevadacityoddfellows.com. Source: nevadacityoddfellows.com; hall rental.

  • Nevada Lodge No. 13, Free & Accepted Masons108 1/2 N. Pine St.

    Meets the second Wednesday at 6:30 PM · (530) 559-0348

    The Masonic lodge on Pine Street, meeting monthly and marching in the Constitution Day parade.

    Lend a hand: Call the lodge secretary to ask about membership. Source: Masons of California lodge listing; Constitution Day parade.

  • Nevada City Little LeagueNevada City

    Spring youth baseball · info@nevadacitybaseball.com

    Spring baseball for Nevada City kids, January through June. Last season, early-bird sign-ups opened November 15 and regular registration ran through January 31.

    Lend a hand: Sign up a player, coach or sponsor through its league site. Source: league site.

  • Nevada County Sheriff's Search & RescueCountywide

    Volunteer rescuers, on call around the clock

    Volunteers who head out at any hour on rescue and recovery missions, working with the Sheriff's Office. Up to 27 of them worked a single day of the February 2026 Castle Peak avalanche response, and the team shared a Distinguished Service Award at the State Capitol in March. Its support nonprofit is based in Nevada City.

    Lend a hand: Ask the Sheriff's Office about the next volunteer academy. Source: Sierra Sun; Gudsy nonprofit listing.

Down the Hill in Grass Valley

Countywide groups based a few miles away that serve Nevada City every day.

  • Bear Yuba Land Trust12183 Auburn Rd., Grass Valley

    (530) 272-5994

    "Saves land, builds trails, and offers programs that get people outdoors": more than 26,000 acres conserved and 55-plus miles of trail built or kept up, including many on our Trails & Vistas page.

    Lend a hand: Join, give or volunteer at bylt.org. Source: bylt.org.

  • Hospitality House1262 Sutton Way, Grass Valley

    Shelter line 530-271-7144

    Runs Utah's Place, the county's emergency shelter, plus housing, respite care and a pet program, "bringing homeless people in Nevada County into a circle of community caring." Cooks are always needed.

    Lend a hand: Volunteer to cook or give at hhshelter.org. Source: hhshelter.org.

  • Food Bank of Nevada CountyWarehouse at 310 Railroad Ave., Grass Valley

    (530) 272-3796

    Hands out groceries around the county, with emergency pickups at the Grass Valley warehouse on weekdays.

    Lend a hand: Call to volunteer or give. Source: California Local directory.

  • Interfaith Food Ministry440 Henderson St., Grass Valley

    (530) 273-8132

    A food pantry sponsored by local churches and run by about 450 volunteers, with no government funding.

    Lend a hand: Call to volunteer or give. Source: California Local directory.

  • Sierra Harvest113 Presley Way, Suite 2A, Grass Valley

    (530) 265-2343

    "Real food. Grown locally. Shared equally." Farm-to-school lessons reach 96 percent of western Nevada County's K-8 students, plus gleaning, farmer help and classes.

    Lend a hand: Volunteer or donate at sierraharvest.org. Source: sierraharvest.org.

  • Sammie's FriendsNevada County Animal Shelter, 14647 McCourtney Rd., Grass Valley

    Shelter Wed-Sat, noon to 5 PM, as posted · adoptions by appointment

    Runs the county animal shelter and pays for medical care that makes its dogs and cats adoptable; its Sammie's Angels drive helps families with pet food and vet bills over the holidays.

    Lend a hand: Adopt, volunteer or give at sammiesfriends.org. Source: sammiesfriends.org.
