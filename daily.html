How the Nugget Works

The Nugget is a daily paper you read on a website. Every morning a fresh edition is set in type, and by 6 AM it is up at nevadacitynugget.com, in place of yesterday's. Same address, new paper, seven days a week.

So the trick is simply to come back. Open it with your morning coffee the way you would pick a paper off the porch. When news breaks, an Evening Edition may follow after 5 PM. Yesterday's papers are kept in the archive.

Never Miss One

1. Have it delivered by email

The morning paper lands in your inbox at about half past six. Free, no trackers, one click to leave. Buttondown keeps the list and sends one email to confirm. The particulars.

2. Put it on your phone

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open nevadacitynugget.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open nevadacitynugget.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app), then Add.

The Nugget's icon then sits with your apps. Tap it each morning and the new paper is there.

3. Make it your computer's first page

Bookmark it: press Ctrl+D (Windows) or ⌘+D (Mac) while you are on the front page.

Or open it every time your browser starts. In Chrome: Settings, then On startup, then Open a specific page, and add nevadacitynugget.com. In Safari: Settings, then General, and set Homepage to nevadacitynugget.com. In Edge: Settings, then Start, home, and new tabs.

Sign Up a Friend

Know someone who would like the Nugget waiting in their inbox each morning? Put their address in and Buttondown will ask them.

Buttondown sends your friend one email asking them to confirm. Nothing else arrives unless they click to say yes, and the Nugget never sees or keeps the address.

Telling someone in person? Here is the whole pitch: "It's a free local paper online. Go to nevadacitynugget.com any morning and there's a new one."
