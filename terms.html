Reading the Paper

Not an emergency service

The paper reports fires, weather, road closures, and power shutoffs, and may print an EXTRA when something serious is under way. It is a newspaper, not an alert system, and it can be hours behind events. In an emergency follow the official sources (Nevada County OES, CAL FIRE, the National Weather Service, CHP, PG&E) and call 911 when lives or property are at risk.

Accuracy, and its limits

The paper works hard to be right, links every story to its source, and prints corrections when it is wrong. Even so, it is provided as is, without any warranty. Showtimes, hours, menus, prices, and event details change; check with the venue or business before you go. Summaries of meetings, agendas, and public notices are a reader's guide, not the official record, which is kept by the city or county.

Entertainment

The Stars Over Deer Creek is entertainment and the paper's only invented content. Recipes are offered in good faith; cook to your own judgment and dietary needs.

Not legal, medical, or financial advice

Nothing in the paper is professional advice. Reporting on an ordinance, a permit, or a rate change explains what was decided, not what you should do about it.
