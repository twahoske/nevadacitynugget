The Visitor's Bar Guide

Where Can I Get a Damn Drink Around Here?

Nevada City Nugget · Saloons, Lounges, Taps & Tastings

◂ Back to Today's Edition

Most of Nevada City's bars sit within a short walk of Broad Street, so park the car once and leave it. Hours are as each place posts them; call ahead, because foothill hours shift with the season.

Last checked October 28, 2026

Saloons & Late Bars

Where the night runs long, some of it past 1 AM.

  • Crazy Horse Saloon & Grill230 Commercial St.

    Mon-Fri noon-midnight · Sat noon-2 AM · Sun 10 AM-2 AM

    Pub grub in a 19th-century building, full bar, live bands and the game on. The publisher vouches for the wings and the tater tots. Source: Google listing.

  • Mine Shaft Saloon222 Broad St.

    Mon-Thu 7 AM-1 AM · Fri-Sat 7 AM-1:30 AM · Sun 10 AM-1 AM · (530) 265-6310

    The publisher calls it the best dive bar in town: small, no-frills, with a patio and a reputation for strong, cheap drinks. Same owners as Fred's Chinese next door, and you can eat Fred's right at the bar. Source: Google listing, the publisher, and the Nevada County LocalWiki.

  • The Fern235 Commercial St.

    Mon & Wed 4 PM-2 AM · Tue 4:30 PM-2 AM · Thu-Sun 3 PM-2 AM · (530) 817-9919

    Late-night cocktail bar in the old Cooper's space, with outdoor seating and live music; shows and DJ nights usually start at 9 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Source: Google listing.

  • Trailhead Tavern315 Broad St.

    Coming soon; hours not yet posted

    New bar in the old McGee's space that bills itself as "your Nevada City basecamp": a beer after a bike ride or a day at the river, with live music, karaoke and trivia nights planned. Source: Google listing and the publisher.

Cocktail Lounges & Hotel Bars

Stirred, not shaken, under old ceilings.

  • Golden Era309 Broad St.

    Wed 4-10 PM · Thu 4-11 PM · Fri-Sat 4 PM-midnight · Sun from 4 PM · Mon-Tue closed · (530) 264-7048

    Saloon-style cocktail lounge in the old Cirino's building: a golden ceiling, a piano, an 1890s back bar, house-made syrups and bitters, and live music most weekends. No reservations; outside food is welcome. Source: goldeneralounge.com.

  • The National Bar211 Broad St., National Exchange Hotel

    Mon-Thu noon-10 PM · Fri noon-11 PM · Sat 9 AM-11 PM · Sun 9 AM-10 PM

    The hotel's historic, dark-paneled tavern, which calls itself "a Legendary Haunt": draft and bottled beer, wine, classic and craft cocktails, small bites. Source: thenationalexchangehotel.com.

  • The River Downtown232 Commercial St.

    Mon, Thu-Sat 4-9 PM · Sun 11:30 AM-8 PM · Tue-Wed closed

    Family-owned, self-described "neighborhood watering hole": cocktails, local craft drinks, shareable plates, a patio. Source: theriverdowntown.com.

  • Friar Tuck's Restaurant & Bar111 N. Pine St.

    From 5 PM nightly; Wednesdays vary, so call · (530) 265-9093

    Old-timey chophouse with a proper bar: cocktails, happy hour bites, live music, and fondue if you stay for dinner. Source: friartucks.com.

  • Deer Creek Bar & Grill101 Broad St.

    Daily 9 AM-9 PM · (530) 298-6778

    Casual bar and grill at the foot of Broad with a patio overlooking Deer Creek: cocktails, burgers and sandwiches, and breakfast from 9 AM. The old Lefty's spot. Source: dcbargrill.com.

Brewpubs & Taprooms

Beer made here, or poured by the people who care.

  • Three Forks Bakery & Brewing211 Commercial St.

    Sun, Mon, Wed, Thu 8 AM-9 PM · Fri-Sat 8 AM-10 PM · Tue closed (winter hours)

    House-brewed IPAs, lagers and seasonal ales with wood-fired sourdough pizza. Family-friendly. Source: threeforksnc.com.

  • BrewBilt Brewhaus300 Spring St.

    Daily 11 AM-9 PM (kitchen to 8:30)

    German-style beer hall and beer garden pouring its own Kölsch, IPA and lagers. Families and dogs welcome. Source: brewbiltbrewhaus.com.

  • Jernigan's Tap House & Grill123 Argall Way

    Mon 3-8 PM · Tue-Sat 11:30 AM-9 PM · Sun 11:30 AM-8 PM

    Taps and burgers out on Argall Way, a short drive from downtown. Source: jernigansgrill.com.

  • Northridge773 Nevada St.

    Mon-Thu 11 AM-8:30 PM · Fri 11-9 · Sat 11-8:30 · Sun 11-8 · Happy hour 2-4 PM

    Casual, family-style comfort food and fresh pizza, sports on the screens, and an afternoon happy hour. Source: northridgenevadacity.com.

Wine Tasting Rooms

Sierra Foothills wine, poured in town. Afternoons only.

  • Nevada City Winery321 Spring St.

    Mon-Wed 2-6 PM · Thu 2-7 PM · Fri-Sat noon-7 PM · Sun noon-6 PM

    Downtown winery with a walk-in tasting bar; closed Sat., Nov. 14 for a private event. Source: ncwinery.com.

  • Szabo Vineyards316 Broad St.

    Fri 2-7:30 PM · Sat-Sun 1-7 PM · Mon-Thu closed

    Small-production Rhône-style wines "with Hungarian roots," poured by the tasting, glass or bottle. (Szabo is next door to The Hat Store, which the publisher owns; there is no business tie.) Source: szabovineyards.com.

Out on the Highway

Worth the drive. Pick a driver.

  • The Willo16898 Hwy. 49

    Thu-Mon: bar from 1 PM, dining room 5-9 PM · Tue-Wed closed · (530) 265-9902

    Classic roadhouse bar beside the steakhouse: cocktails, whiskey, the game on TV, full menu at the bar after 5. A local favorite. Source: thewillo.com.

Drink like a local. Downtown is hilly and the sidewalks are old brick, so wear sensible shoes. Bring ID. Don't drink and drive these mountain roads.

Bar owners: missing, closed, or wrong hours? Write news@nevadacitynugget.com. Nobody pays to be listed here.
