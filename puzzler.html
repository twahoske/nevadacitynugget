How To Play

Three pans a day. The Assay is a four-way question. True or Tall Tale? asks whether a claim holds up or is campfire talk. The Scramble is a local word with its letters stirred. Each good pan earns a gold nugget.

The streak. Finish the day's puzzle on days in a row to build your Puzzler streak. Miss a day and a new run starts; your best stays on the books. Back issues are free to play but don't count.

Every answer is checked. Each day's facts come from a document named at the bottom of the puzzle, mostly National Register nominations and old newspapers. The tall tales are ours, made up on purpose, and each one is set straight the moment you answer. Spot an error? news@nevadacitynugget.com.

Kept on your device. Your streak and scores stay in this browser and are never sent to the Nugget or anyone else. No accounts, no leaderboards.
