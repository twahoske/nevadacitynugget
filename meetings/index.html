Unofficial automatic transcript

This text was made by machine from YouTube's automatic captions of the city's own videos (the channel Nevada City Public Meetings). It has not been checked word by word, speakers are not labeled, and it will contain misheard words and misspelled names. For readability, "um" and "uh" were taken out, caption lines were joined into paragraphs, and a few common mishearings were fixed (such as "Nissan" for Nisenan); everything else is as captioned. Bracketed notes like [laughter] come from the captions.

It is not the official record. The city's adopted minutes and the video are. Each timestamp opens the video at that moment, so check any quote there before you repeat it. Agendas and minutes: City of Nevada City Agenda Center.
