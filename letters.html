How to Write the Nugget

The paper prints letters from its readers, the old-fashioned way. Say your piece about Nevada City, the county, or something you read in the Nugget, in 200 words or fewer. Send it to tips@nevadacitynugget.com with "Letter to the Editor" in the subject line, and sign it with your full name and the town you live in. Letters that disagree with the Nugget are welcome.

The Rules

  1. Short. 200 words or fewer. Longer letters are returned or trimmed.
  2. Signed. Your full name and your town, both printed. No anonymous letters, no pen names, and no letters signed only by a group or business.
  3. Your own words. Written for the Nugget by you: no form letters, petitions, or copies of a letter sent to every paper in the county.
  4. One a month. One letter per writer every 30 days, so more neighbors get a turn.
  5. About here. Nevada City and western Nevada County, its public life, and what the Nugget has printed.
  6. Ideas, not people. Argue as hard as you like with a decision or an opinion. Letters with name-calling, personal attacks, accusations of crimes that are not on the public record, or anything defamatory, obscene, or hateful toward people for who they are do not run.
  7. No selling. A letter that advertises a business, plugs an event, or raises money is not a letter. Send events to the same address as a listing instead.
  8. Elections. Letters about candidates and ballot measures stop running one week before Election Day, so no one gets the last word without a chance to answer.
  9. Facts are checked. A letter may state an opinion, but a claim of fact is checked like any story. One that cannot be checked is held or trimmed.

How Letters Are Chosen and Edited

The publisher decides. Every letter that prints has been read and approved by the publisher. Not every letter runs, and the paper does not promise to print any one of them. The paper's compiling software sorts the mail and sets approved letters in type; it never decides what runs.

Light edits only. Letters may be trimmed for length and corrected for spelling, grammar, and house style. A letter's meaning is never changed. If a bigger cut is needed, the paper asks you first.

We may write back. Before a letter prints, the paper may write to the address it came from to confirm that you sent it.

Headings. The paper writes the short heading over each letter.

Links and attachments. Letters print as text only. Links, photographs, and attachments are not printed.

Answering back. Anyone named or criticized in a letter may answer with a letter of their own, under the same rules.

What Gets Printed

Your letter, your name, and your town, in the paper, its morning email edition, and its archive, where printed editions stay as they ran. You keep the copyright in your letter; sending it gives the Nugget permission to print it there. Your email address and anything else you send along are never printed. Letters are the writers' own views, not the paper's. How your message is handled is on the Privacy page.

To report an error in the paper itself, write news@nevadacitynugget.com as before; corrections run at the bottom of the next edition.
