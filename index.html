County · Animal Services · Comment Through Nov. 30

One Week Left To Weigh In On The New County Animal Shelter

Written comments on the environmental document for Nevada County's planned animal shelter at 12270 La Barr Meadows Road, south of Grass Valley, are due by 5 PM next Monday, Nov. 30. The project is estimated at about $17 million, and the county has asked Nevada City and Grass Valley, which share the aging Freeman Lane shelter, to join a regional facility, The Union reported.

The Mitigated Negative Declaration flags potentially significant impacts on biological, cultural and tribal cultural resources, noise, and utilities. Comments go to Assistant Planner Aleena Church, county Planning Department, 950 Maidu Ave., Suite 170, Nevada City. The county Zoning Administrator takes final action after a hearing not yet scheduled.

Source: The Union, Nov. 19.

Election · Measure Q · Next Count Wednesday

Measure Q Holds At 60 Percent As The Count Pauses For The Holiday

Measure Q, the short-term rental permit measure, still leads 967 Yes (59.99 percent) to 645 No (40.01 percent) with all four city precincts reporting. The county's next count is Wednesday afternoon, and certification is due Dec. 3.

Read the whole story

Nevada City's Measure Q, the short-term rental permit measure, starts Thanksgiving week where Friday's count left it: 967 Yes (59.99 percent) to 645 No (40.01 percent), with all four city precincts reporting, according to Nevada County Elections figures posted by YubaNet. The county has scheduled no count before Wednesday.

What is left. Friday's update counted 849 more ballots countywide and left about 850 still to be processed and 273 waiting on voters to cure signature or other problems. The county does not break those remaining ballots out by city, and its results page does not mark the measure as decided.

What comes next. The next update is set for Wednesday, Nov. 25, in the late afternoon. Every figure is unofficial until the county certifies the election; the state deadline is Dec. 3. Measure Q is the only Nevada City contest on the ballot.

Sources: Nevada County Elections via YubaNet, "Election Results Update Nov. 20"; Nevada County Elections, Nov. 2.

County · Assessor · Annual Report

Where Your Property Tax Dollar Goes

By the county Assessor's 2026 annual report, each property tax dollar goes roughly 55 cents to schools, 20 cents to special districts, 14 cents to county services, and 11 cents to Nevada City, Grass Valley and Truckee together.

Read the whole story

Assessor Rolf D. Kleinhans released the report last week. It cites a 99.83 percent assessment ratio from the State Board of Equalization's 2024-25 survey of the office's work. Printed copies are at the Government Center, 950 Maidu Ave., Suite 290, in Nevada City; the office's number is 530-265-1232.

County · Code Enforcement · Comment Through Dec. 16

Comment Continues On A County Public Nuisance Ordinance

Nevada County is taking comment through Dec. 16 on a proposed Public Nuisance Abatement Ordinance, a new Chapter 7.02 of the county code. It would apply in the unincorporated county, not inside Nevada City.

Read the whole story

The draft defines nuisances such as unsafe structures, fire hazards, illegal dumping, and recurring noise or traffic problems; the county says it creates no new zoning or building standards and would seek voluntary compliance first. A first reading before the Board of Supervisors is expected in January 2027. Comments go to CodeCompliance@nevadacountyca.gov with "Public Nuisance Abatement Ordinance" in the subject line. Separately, the county Recorder's document fees rise Jan. 1, 2027, the first increase since 2010 (Nevada County, Nov. 17).

Source: Nevada County via The Union, Nov. 19.

One Dry Day, Then A Milder Storm Than First Feared

Dry today · Showers, breezes and a chance of thunder Tuesday and Wednesday · Snow only above about 5,000 to 7,000 feet · Showers linger Thursday

Hey Good Lookin', Whatcha Got Cookin'

Roasted Delicata Squash With Cranberries, Cashews And An Espresso-Maple Glaze (a Nugget original). Heat the oven to 425. Halve two delicata squash lengthwise, scoop out the seeds, and slice them into half-inch half-moons; no need to peel. Toss them with two tablespoons of olive oil, a good pinch of salt and a few grinds of pepper, spread them on a sheet pan in one layer, and roast about 25 minutes, turning once, until browned at the edges. Meanwhile, stir together two tablespoons of maple syrup, a teaspoon of finely ground espresso or a tablespoon of strong brewed espresso, a tablespoon of butter, and a pinch of cayenne in a small pan over low heat until the butter melts. In the last five minutes of roasting, scatter a half cup of fresh cranberries and a half cup of raw cashews over the squash and drizzle on the glaze, then return the pan to the oven until the cranberries just begin to split. Finish with a squeeze of orange and a handful of chopped parsley. It serves four as a side, honors three of today's observances at once, and is the only dish on the Thanksgiving table that will keep you awake through the football.
