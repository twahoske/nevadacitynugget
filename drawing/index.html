Sign Up to Enter

Every subscriber to the free daily Nugget is entered in a monthly drawing for a gift card to a Nevada City shop. This month: a $100 gift card, drawn December 21.

Free. One email a morning, one click to leave. Buttondown, which keeps the list, sends one email asking you to confirm.

What you get. Every morning by 6, the day's paper: City Hall and the county, fire and weather, the Docket and the blotter, what's playing tonight, who's on the radio, and the stars over Deer Creek. Signing up also enters you in the monthly drawing.

Official Rules

December 2026 drawing

  1. No purchase necessary. Signing up is free, and buying anything does not improve your chances.
  2. Who can enter. Anyone 18 or older living in the United States. The publisher, the publisher's household, and Hat Store staff cannot win. Void where prohibited.
  3. How to enter. Be a confirmed email subscriber to the Nevada City Nugget by 11:59 PM Pacific on Sunday, December 20, 2026. Everyone already on the list is entered automatically. Or enter without subscribing: email news@nevadacitynugget.com with the subject "Drawing" by the same deadline. One entry per person, however you enter.
  4. The prize. One $100 gift card to a downtown Nevada City shop, named on this page by December 1, 2026. No cash in place of the prize.
  5. The drawing. On Monday, December 21, 2026, one winner is picked at random from all entries. Odds depend on how many people enter.
  6. Winning. The winner is told by email and has 7 days to reply, or a new winner is drawn. With the winner's OK, the paper prints their first name and town. Nothing else about any entrant is published or shared.
  7. Every month after. A new drawing each month; its prize and dates are posted on this page before it starts.
  8. Run by the Nevada City Nugget, Nevada City, California. Questions: news@nevadacitynugget.com.
