The Particulars

What you get

One email a morning with the day's paper, from news@nevadacitynugget.com. On days when news breaks, an Evening Edition or an Extra may follow, and only when something real happened.

What it costs

Nothing. The Nugget does not sell, rent, or trade the list, and no one pays to reach it.

What the list keeps

Your email address, when you signed up, and whether you are still subscribed. The paper does not track whether you open its emails or which links you click. The full account is on the privacy page.

Leaving

Click "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any issue, or write news@nevadacitynugget.com and we will take you off by hand.
