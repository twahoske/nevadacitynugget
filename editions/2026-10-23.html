City Hall & Politics

Election · November 3

Early Voting Opens Saturday At The Rood Center

Today is the last day at the Loma Rica processing center · 18 drop boxes countywide · Mail by Tuesday, elections officials advise

In-person voting for the November 3 general election opens Saturday, October 24 at the Nevada County Government Center's Providence Mine Room, 950 Maidu Ave., a short walk from downtown. Hours run 9 AM to 5 PM daily through November 2, then 7 AM to 8 PM on Election Day. Today, Friday, is the final day of in-person voting at the Ballot Processing Center at 12740 Loma Rica Drive in Grass Valley; once the Rood Center opens, all in-person services move there.

The vote center handles registration, replacement ballots, accessible voting equipment, language assistance, and ballot drop-off, plus the county's "Sign, Scan and Go" option for voters who fill out a ballot at home and scan it themselves. Eighteen official drop boxes are open countywide, mapped at nevadacountyca.gov/votemap. The Elections Office encourages anyone mailing a ballot to do it by Tuesday, October 27: postage is paid, only ballots postmarked by November 3 count, and outbound mail is postmarked in Sacramento or Reno, which takes time. Ballots can be tracked at WheresMyBallot.sos.ca.gov, and the county streams its ballot processing live on the Elections Office YouTube channel.

Source: Nevada County Elections Office via YubaNet, Oct 21

Conditions (updated 5 PM)

Rain Exits, Wind Arrives: A Dry North-To-East Blow For The Weekend

The week's showers and isolated thunderstorms wind down this morning, with snow forecast above 8,000 feet in the high Sierra, and the National Weather Service expects increasing north-to-east winds tonight into Saturday with a drying trend behind them, per guidance relayed Thursday. After a damp week, highs recover to near 68 over the weekend. Dry wind over fresh fuel is the fall pattern that keeps engines busy: the county's burn ban remains in effect, and crews spent the week chasing burn piles from Lake Vera to McCourtney Road, one of which earned its owner a citation.

PG&E has announced no Public Safety Power Shutoff for this wind event as of press time, and its shutoff page remained closed out at a 5 PM check. The utility's October 14 to 17 shutoff, which de-energized 2,137 Nevada County customers at its peak, was fully restored October 17. NWS morning guidance, relayed Friday, rates the wind's impact minor and the strongest Sierra northeast-to-east winds Friday evening into Saturday morning, adding that recent rains and lingering low-level moisture should mitigate fire weather concerns to some extent; secure outdoor items and expect downed branches.

Sources: NWS Sacramento guidance via YubaNet's Happening Now log; PG&E newsroom; temperatures via OpenSnow, Oct 23.

Evening Update · Arts

City Opens A Call For Arts Partners At The CHAMP Gallery

The city and its Public Arts Commission put out a request for proposals today seeking arts organizations, nonprofits, artist collectives, and community groups to mount two to three exhibitions a year at the CHAMP Gallery inside City Hall. Two models are offered: organization-led shows, with a stipend of up to $1,000 for qualifying groups, and city-supported shows where staff help with installation, promotion, and the reception but no stipend. Gallery space is free either way, and the city says it especially welcomes proposals featuring local and regional artists, regional history and culture, youth engagement, or emerging artists. "We have an incredible creative community, and we want to give more organizations an opportunity to showcase their artists," City Manager Carrie Wright said. Proposals are due November 19 at 3 PM to Deputy City Clerk Amber Wright, subject line "CHAMP Gallery Partner Application," with the full RFP at nevadacityca.gov; later submissions are kept for future rounds.

Source: The Union, Oct 23

Hey Good Lookin', Whatcha Got Cookin'

Burn-Ban Baked Apples (a Nugget original, debuting today) — Core four firm apples, Mutsu or Granny Smith, leaving the bottoms intact. Stuff each with a mix of a third-cup brown sugar, a quarter-cup chopped walnuts, two tablespoons soft butter, a teaspoon of cinnamon, a handful of rolled oats, and a pinch of salt. Set them in a baking dish with a half-inch of apple cider, bake at 375° for 35 to 40 minutes until tender, and spoon the pan syrup over the tops. Cream optional, recommended. With outdoor burning banned countywide, this is the only thing you should be burning this weekend. Readers' own recipes are welcome through the house channel and run with credit and permission.
