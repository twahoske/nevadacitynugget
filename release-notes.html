News from the Composing Room

◂ Back to Today's Paper

Beta Mode  The Nugget is new, and still being built. The paper first went to press on October 22, 2026, and new features are added most days. Expect the odd smudge while the ink dries; mistakes are corrected at the bottom of the next edition.

The version number (v0.9.10 today) goes up by a tenth when a big batch of new features lands, and by a point (v0.9.1, v0.9.2) for smaller additions in between. The daily editions themselves don't change it. The paper stays in beta for now.

Found something broken, or have an idea? Write news@nevadacitynugget.com.

Version 0.9.10

The TrimWednesday, November 18, 2026

  • A shorter paper. The Nugget had grown to about 49 phone screens, and only a dozen of them were news. It now runs about 34. Nothing was thrown out: some things moved next door to the Visitor Guide, some boxes were combined, and the long lists now start folded.
  • Tap to unfold. The Docket's regional agendas, the Civic Calendar past the coming week, the full Radio Log with hosts, and standing legal notices now show a one-line label. Tap it and the whole thing unfolds, every word still there. City Council and Planning Commission agendas stay open in full.
  • Three stories in full, the rest in brief. City Hall & Politics prints its top three stories whole. Any others run as a headline and two sentences, with "Read the whole story" right under them for readers who want all the goods.
  • City Hall on the Record. Four small boxes (Elected Officials, Meetings on the Record, From the Dais and Meeting Watch) are now one, led by the day's quote from the dais, with links to the Officials' Page and every meeting transcript.
  • The Holiday Table moved. Where to eat on Thanksgiving, and where to find a free plate, now lives in the Visitor Guide along with the full Red Dot Winter Art Market notice. The paper's "Visiting?" box lists what is in the Guide each day.
  • Smaller odds and ends. The countdown to the big events is now one line atop the Civic Calendar, The Nugget's Guides is a single line of links, and Previously lists three headlines without the summaries.

Version 0.9.9

Finding Your WayTuesday, November 17, 2026

  • An easier way back to the paper. The "Back to today's paper" link under the Visitor Guide nameplate was a faint gray whisper. It now prints in bold black ink (bright white in dark mode), so it's easy to spot on any screen.
  • In This Guide. A one-line index now runs under the Visitor Guide nameplate: Eat, Drink, Walk, Stay and More Guides. Tap one and you land right on that section.
  • More Guides, all in one place. The bottom of the Visitor Guide now has a shelf of every Nugget guide (the Bar Guide, Trails & Vistas, Where to Stay, Tonics & Tinctures, Good Works, Victorian Christmas and the day trips), plus big buttons back to today's paper and to the top. The paper gets the same shelf, "The Nugget's Guides," near its end, with the Visitor Guide, Elected Officials, Meetings on the Record and the Archive added.
  • One way home. Every guide and reference page now says the same thing on its button: "Back to Today's Paper."

Version 0.9.8

Good Works, Tonics & A Tidier TopMonday, November 16, 2026

  • Tonics & Tinctures: The Nevada City Wellness Guide. Step right up! Seventeen of the town's apothecaries, elixir bars, limbering parlors, healers, rock shops and more, each described in its own words with a wink from the medicine wagon. The Nugget cures nothing, not even Mondays.
  • The Daily Restorative starts Thursday, Nov. 19: one wellness establishment spotlighted each morning, with a drawn medicine bottle and the rest of the cabinet listed underneath. No one pays to appear.
  • Good Works: Nevada City's Nonprofits, a standing guide to 33 local nonprofits, from the Miners Foundry and the Narrow Gauge Railroad Museum to SYRCL, Child Advocates and the Odd Fellows: what each does, where to find it, and how to give, join or pitch in.
  • Nonprofit of the Day starts Tuesday, Dec. 1, Giving Tuesday: one group profiled each morning, right after Merchant of the Day, through the year-end giving season. No one pays to appear.
  • A roomier paper early in the week. The Red Dot Gallery's Winter Art Market notice now runs Wednesday through Sunday only, through Dec. 20, matching the days the gallery is open for the market.
  • Drawings on the Visitor Guide. Every section now opens under a slim illustrated band, drawn for the Nugget: a covered dish for the Bill of Fare, batwing doors for the Bar Guide, ridgelines for Trails & Vistas, and more. They switch to light ink in dark mode. On phones, the Bill of Fare also lost the blank gaps under each meal.
  • A tidier top of the page. The front page had collected a pile of notices above the news. The classic nameplate stays, the Beta badge shrinks to a small stamp, and the day's lead headline now runs big and bold on the first screen. The email sign-up, Total Circulation and the Inside strip moved to just under the lead story, and a slim nameplate bar follows you down the page with a quick way back to the top. The red line over a story no longer repeats its section's name, and on wide screens the box to the right of the nameplate now links both today's paper and the Visitor Guide.
  • Two gold nuggets a day. The Hidden Nugget now has company: the Morning Nugget hides in the paper all day, and the Evening Nugget turns up somewhere else after 5 PM, when the Evening Edition lands. Strike gold and the nugget bursts into gold dust and the Assay Office hands you a receipt, stamped STAKED. Tapping a nugget you've already found now tells you so (it used to do nothing). Finds still stay in your own browser.
  • City Hall in one place. The Elected Officials box now sits directly above Meetings on the Record, so who serves, what they said at the dais and what is coming up on the agenda read top to bottom together.
  • A gold nugget in your browser tab. The Nugget finally has its own little icon: a gold nugget on a black square, drawn for the paper. Look for it on your browser tab and your bookmarks, and on your phone's home screen if you add the Nugget there. It replaces a faint mountain sketch that some browsers didn't show at all.

Version 0.9.7

Milestones & GamesSunday, November 15, 2026

  • When Total Circulation passes a milestone (500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000, 3,000, 4,000, 5,000, 7,500, 10,000, and on from there), an "Extra!" box with its own drawing appears under the date that day and the next, and the Nugget celebrates on Instagram and Facebook. Whoever happens to be the exact milestone copy gets told so.
  • The count is the same honest one as always: copies of today's paper read at nevadacitynugget.com, one per phone or computer a day (see Circulation Figures).
  • Road to the next milestone: a little progress bar under the date shows how many copies to go.
  • Prospector's Streak: "Day 6 at the claim" counts the mornings in a row you've read the paper, with ranks at 7, 30 and 100 days (Greenhorn, Sourdough, Old-Timer).
  • The Hidden Nugget: a tiny gold nugget is tucked somewhere different in each edition. Find it and tap it. Your streak and finds stay in your own browser (see Privacy).
  • Wild & Scenic, on the beat: the Nugget now keeps watch on SYRCL's Wild & Scenic Film Festival (the 25th runs Feb. 18 to 22, 2027) every morning: new films, schedules and deadlines as they post, a countdown in Amusements the two weeks before, and a "Plan Ahead" box in the Visitor Guide. First up, Nov. 16 to 18: "Wild & Scenic Turns 25."
