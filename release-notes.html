Beta Mode  The Nugget is new, and still being built. The paper first went to press on October 22, 2026, and new features are added most days. Expect the odd smudge while the ink dries; mistakes are corrected at the bottom of the next edition.

The version number (v0.7 today) goes up by a tenth each time a batch of new features lands. The daily editions themselves don't change it.

Found something broken, or have an idea? Write news@nevadacitynugget.com.

Version 0.7

Meetings on the RecordFriday, October 30, 2026

  • Meetings on the Record (meetings/): searchable transcripts of every City Council and Planning Commission meeting the city posted from July through October 2026, with each timestamp opening the city's video at that moment.
  • They are unofficial, made by machine from YouTube's automatic captions, and say so on every page; the city's minutes remain the official record.
  • Each edition now carries a standing link to the transcripts beside City Hall coverage.

Trails, Beds, and a Stamp of Our OwnWednesday, October 28, 2026

Version 0.2

The First Round of FixesMonday, October 26, 2026

  • New Standards & Disclosures, Privacy, and Terms of Use pages.
  • On phones, the page no longer scrolls sideways, and the Wire button stays inside the column.
  • A redesigned dateline bar: issue number, date, and elevation in three columns.
  • Radio Log: The Playground and The Music Grotto added to Mondays, from KVMR's Spinitron listings.
  • Groundwork to help Google find the paper: a sitemap, search listing data, and archived editions that point to their own addresses.
  • Search and share previews now describe the Nugget as "A Modern-Vintage daily paper for Nevada City, California."

Version 0.1

First EditionThursday, October 22, 2026

  • Edition No. 1: a daily paper in a 1941 broadsheet design, with the blackletter nameplate and ridge engraving.
  • City Hall & Politics leading, The Docket of agenda digests, Legal Notices, and the Police Blotter.
  • Meeting summaries from official recordings, and Elected Officials with sourced quotes from the public record.
  • Amusements with ticket links, the KVMR Radio Log with Listen Live, and The Back Page.
  • The Wire share button on every section, the Sources & Methods page, an edition archive, the tip line, an Evening Edition, and an emergency Extra desk.
